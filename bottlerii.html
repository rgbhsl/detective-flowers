You think Botller is innocent!

"I belive you," you say, "I'm sorry."

Botller begins shaking.

"But I thought...you would've caught me..."

You look at him in confusion, when he reaches into his coat.

"I'm a liar." He sobs.

Diamonds, pearls, sapphires. He pulls out a necklace so extremely laden with polished jewels that you can barely see it under the light.

"I didn't want to do it...I was told to! But I've betrayed the trust of Lady Welthie."

"Who told you to?"

Botller opens his mouth to speak but nothing comes out.

"I can't say her name..."

"Then I'd have to turn you in."
