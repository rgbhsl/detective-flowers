Lady Welthie's Diamonds Stolen!

On May 2nd, 1885, Lady Welthie got home from the theatre, got back to her mansion and had her dinner, and went to get something from her safe, only to discover that almost all her precious jewellery had been stolen! The combination lock was open, and the safe was unharmed otherwise.

Even though she is very wealthy, Lady Welthie could unfortunately not get the best consulting detective in the world, Sherlock Holmes, to help.

"It simply isn't an interesting enough case for me," said Mr. Holmes. "It's too obvious who the criminal is. It's clearly-"

Our reporter, Rob, got promptly attacked by a flock of geese and hence could not get the rest of Mr. Holmes' statement.

Lady Welthie has hired someone she refers to as "Detective Flowers, the second best consulting detective in the world." We do not know who this person is.

Botller

Lady Welthie's butler, Botller, was one of the only people present in the house when she was at the theatre. Botller's family has worked for the Welthie family for generations. Lady Welthie trusts him with everything, including the combination of all her safes.

"It is a dishonor to me to even be considered a suspect." Botller appeared worried when he found out about the theft. "I am disappointed in myself for letting this happen. The Welthie family has always taken good care of my family."

Botller claims he was far away from Lady Welthie's safe, setting up the table in the dining hall for dinner.

"I'm an amazing butler, if I do say so myself," says Botller.

Lady Welthie had her dinner once she got home and said that the table was set up meticulously and perfectly.
