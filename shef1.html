I know who it is!

You suspect Shef!

"Madam Welthie," you drop your voice, "I think the person behind the theft might be Shef."

"Why, whatever do you mean, Detective Flowers? Shef is so highly loyal!" She says. "Would she really...do such a thing?"

"Yes, I do think so. She said something about-" You go quiet as Shef enters the dining hall.

"I've outdone myself this time! It's not every day you can serve the world's second best consulting detective."

You nervously twirl a shiny, delicate fork in your hands upon hearing Shef's excitement. There was a bitter undertone in her voice. Or maybe you imagined it.

The dishes are put on the table, and you and Lady Welthie begin to eat in almost utter silence asides from Musik's discordant piano. The food is nice, definitely nicer than you've ever had.
